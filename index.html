The Problem
Buying materials is broken
in India's growing cities.

Homeowners and contractors in Tier-2/3 cities face a fragmented, offline market with no transparency, no comparison, and no convenience.

🕐

Wasted Time

Buyers visit 4–6 shops just to compare prices for cement or tiles. Half the day gone, and still no certainty on the best deal.

💸

Price Exploitation

Without visibility, buyers pay 15–30% more than market price. Local sellers exploit information asymmetry every day.

📦

No Stock Visibility

You drive to a store only to find the material is out of stock. There's no way to check availability without physically going there.

🗺️

Fragmented Market

Thousands of local stores exist but remain invisible digitally. Buyers can't discover them; sellers can't reach more customers.

🤝

Zero Trust System

No ratings, no reviews, no verified sellers. Buyers rely on word-of-mouth and luck to find a reliable material supplier.

📱

Digital Gap

Digital penetration is rising fast in Tier-2/3 cities, but there's still no structured marketplace for construction commerce.

FAQ
Got questions?
We've got answers.
Is NeeVLY free to use for buyers? ▼

Yes, NeeVLY is completely free for buyers. You can search, compare prices, and place orders without any charges. We only earn a small commission from sellers on completed transactions.

Which cities is NeeVLY launching in first? ▼

We are focused on Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities across Jharkhand, Bihar, and UP for our initial launch. Cities like Ranchi, Patna, Jamshedpur, and Varanasi are our priority targets.

How do sellers get verified on NeeVLY? ▼

Sellers go through a simple verification process that includes business document upload, phone verification, and a manual review by our team. Verified sellers get a badge that builds buyer trust.

What payment methods are supported? ▼

At launch, we support Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI payments. We plan to add card payments, wallets, and buy-now-pay-later options in subsequent releases.

Can a seller list multiple stores on NeeVLY? ▼

Yes. Sellers with multiple store locations can manage all of them from a single NeeVLY seller account, with separate storefronts, inventory, and order management for each location.

When is NeeVLY launching? ▼

We are currently in the pre-launch phase building our seller and buyer base. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first to access NeeVLY when we go live in your city.
